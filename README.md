# THE WORLD IN ONE DAY — 19 November 2026

A newspaper front page that sums up the world's news of Thursday 19 November 2026 in one 1024×1024 image, drawn procedurally by `main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only, no network, deterministic).

Lead story: Trump, Xi and Putin hold the first-ever trilateral summit in Shenzhen, with nuclear arms on the agenda. Also: the world court finds Myanmar guilty of genocide against the Rohingya, Ukraine hits the Kerch Bridge a third time in four days, Anthropic sets the record for the biggest IPO ever at $81.3bn, and climate talks stall in Antalya as the IEA warns of a historic energy threat.

Run it:

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
